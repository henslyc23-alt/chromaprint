The studio

A print floor that
never sleeps

Chroma·Print started with a stubborn belief that small objects should feel like an event — and a refusal to sell anything grey. Today it's a six-machine print floor turning out articulated dragons, desk gear and custom pieces for people who would rather own something weird than something off a shelf.

Everything you see here is designed, sliced and printed in-house. Nothing is drop-shipped, nothing sits in a warehouse, and if a print comes out wrong it goes in the recycle bin instead of your parcel.
